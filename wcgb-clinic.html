Where your dollars go

This is bigger than a clinic.

The Crimson & Cream Classic is the flagship fundraiser of Bay Area Community Project Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Together with the Berkeley Alumni Chapter of Kappa Alpha Psi and the Oakland East Bay Alumnae Chapter of Delta Sigma Theta, it funds scholarships for Bay Area students — 100% of net proceeds go to scholarships.
