Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This website, crimsonclassic.org, is operated by Bay Area Community Project Inc. ("BACP," "we," "us"), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, to support the Crimson & Cream Charity Golf Classic. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit the site or register for the event, and how we use it.

Information you provide

When you register for the tournament, clinic, or banquet, sponsor the event, or make a donation, you provide information such as your name, email address, company or organization, and payment details. Payments are processed by Stripe; we do not receive or store your full card number. Sponsors may also upload logos and marketing materials, which we use for event signage, the program, our website, and event communications.

Information collected automatically

We use standard web analytics and advertising tools that collect information about your visit through cookies and similar technologies, including pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type:

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics — privacy-focused site analytics.
  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel — helps us measure the effectiveness of our advertising and show relevant ads about the event on Facebook and Instagram, including to previous visitors of this site. You can learn about Meta's data practices at facebook.com/privacy/policy and control the ads you see at facebook.com/adpreferences.

How we use information

  • To process registrations, sponsorships, and donations, and send confirmations and receipts.
  • To communicate event details, updates, and follow-ups.
  • To recognize sponsors in event materials.
  • To measure and improve our outreach and advertising.

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your information with third parties except the service providers needed to run the event (payment processing, email delivery, analytics, and advertising as described above).

Your choices

  • You can opt out of our emails at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.
  • You can limit ad tracking through your browser settings, and through Meta's ad preferences.
  • California residents may request access to or deletion of their personal information by contacting us at the address below.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: dbohler@crimsonclassic.org.
