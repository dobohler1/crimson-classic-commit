Morning · Tournament

Tee it up early.

Four-person scramble, shotgun start. Greens fees, cart, range balls, lunch on the course, and your banquet seat are all included.

For tournament golfers
  1. 9:00 AM
    Check-in and warm-up
    Pick up your name tag, cart assignment and gift bag, then head to the range and putting green. Coffee and breakfast bites at cart staging. Mulligan and raffle bundles are on sale at the table; buy them now, there's no second chance once you're on the course.
    Registration · practice facility
  2. 10:15 AM
    Welcome and opening remarks
    Group photo, then a short welcome from the co-chairs and the Head Pro on format, pace of play and the contest holes.
    Cart staging
  3. 10:30 AM
    Shotgun start
    All groups tee off at once. Lunch is served on the course. Look for the cigar lounge, the wellness station and the contest holes along the way. When your round ends, scorecards go to the golf shop and cocktail hour is waiting.
    Course
Midday · Golf Clinic + Banquet

New to golf? Start here.

A coached clinic with West Coast Golf Babes, all skill levels, clubs provided. Your ticket flows straight into the evening.

For clinic guests
  1. 1:00 PM
    Clinic check-in
    Check in at registration, then wander over to the range or the wellness station until the clinic begins.
    Registration
  2. 1:30 PM
    Clinic begins
    About 90 minutes with Tori James and the West Coast Golf Babes instructors. No experience needed. Clubs are provided.
    Practice facility
  3. 3:00 PM
    Clinic concludes
    Move up to the clubhouse for a first look at the auction pieces as they're staged.
    Members' lounge
  4. 3:30 PM
    Join the evening
    Cocktail hour begins and the golfers come off the course. From here, your day runs with everyone else's. See below.
    Members' lounge · terrace
Evening · Banquet & Live Black Art Auction

The evening that ties it all together.

Cocktail hour, then the banquet: dinner, sponsor recognition, golf awards, and a live Black art auction that funds Bay Area scholars. Golf attire is welcome all evening; no change of clothes needed.

Everyone
  1. 3:30 PM
    Cocktail hour and silent auction open
    No-host bar, photo backdrop, and the silent auction opens for bidding. Sponsors, this is the moment for photos with your signage before the room fills.
    Members' lounge · terrace
  2. 4:30 PM
    Banquet and art auction
    Seated at 4:30: dinner, sponsor recognition, the raffle, golf awards, and the live Black art auction with fund-a-need.
    Ballroom
  3. 6:30 PM
    Closing and send-off
    Silent auction closes, the final raffle prize is drawn, and checkout opens in the lounge for auction winners. Pieces go home with you tonight.
    Ballroom · lounge

Good to know

👔

Golf attire all day

Collared shirts, no metal spikes on the course. What you play in is what you wear to dinner. No change of clothes needed.

🍽️

What's included

Tournament golfers get lunch on the course and a banquet seat. Clinic guests eat at the banquet; no midday meal is served. Bar is no-host all evening.

🎟️

Bring a card

Mulligans, raffle bundles and the Vegas hole are sold at check-in. Auction checkout takes cards. Buy your mulligans before the shotgun.

📍

Getting there

Stonebrae Country Club, 202 Country Club Dr, Hayward, CA 94542. Follow the drive up to the clubhouse; registration is at the front entrance.

🎨

Art goes home tonight

Auction pieces are released against payment at the end of the evening. Nothing stays overnight, so plan to take your winnings with you.

✉️

Questions

Email dbohler@crimsonclassic.org. Times are as planned on September 25; we'll update this page if anything moves.

Not registered yet? There's still a way in.

The tournament is sold out, but the Golf Clinic + Banquet ticket puts you on the range at 1:30 and in the room by 3:30.
