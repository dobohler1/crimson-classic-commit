A community, not just a crowd

This is not a rented mailing list. The Crimson & Cream Golf Classic is co-hosted by two storied Black Greek-letter organizations — the Berkeley Alumni Chapter of Kappa Alpha Psi and the Oakland East Bay Alumnae Chapter of Delta Sigma Theta — together with Bay Area Community Projects. These are networks built over decades of shared achievement. The people in the room know each other, trust each other, and show up for each other.

"A brand that earns its place here isn't buying impressions. It's being introduced — by a community that vouches for who it lets in."

That trust is why more than a quarter of our guests return every year, and why a partner brand is met with goodwill rather than ad-blindness. You are not interrupting this audience. You are being welcomed into it.

† What's measured vs. estimated. Audience size and return rate come straight from four years of registration records. The seniority and income figures describe a verified sample of attendees, profiled through public records — a leadership sample, not the entire room. Public salary data places those roles at $250,000–$300,000 in salary, and their mostly dual-earner households above $375,000 — roughly 2.5–3 times the Bay Area's median household income of about $135,000 (U.S. Census, 2024). These are sourced estimates for the verified leadership tier, not a survey of every guest; a registrant survey now in the field will extend them to the full room.
